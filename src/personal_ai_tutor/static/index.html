Privacy and retention

This tutor keeps learner interactions, mastery estimates, semantic notes, and safety events scoped to one learner ID. The demo stores learner tokens and parent keys as hashes on the local SQLite database and never stores raw credentials.

  • Interactions, semantic memory, and safety events: deleted after 365 days.
  • Audit records: deleted after 730 days.
  • Idle sessions: deleted after 30 days. Inactive learners are purged after 365 days.
  • Parents can export or delete all learner data from the Progress tab.
  • Safety events record categories and hashes, not raw self-harm or personal information.
